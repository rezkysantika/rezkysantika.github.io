// welcome

Hi, I'm Rezky

I'm a first-year Computer Science PhD student at the University of Minnesota–Twin Cities, advised by Prof. Qianwen Wang in the Visual Intelligent Lab.

I'm currently interested in studying transparency in human–AI interaction that goes both ways: transparency on human intent and transparency on the AI process, and how we can foster better collaboration.

I obtained my bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering from Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember (ITS), Indonesia. I also worked in the PRIME Lab with Prof. Yan Chen at Virginia Tech in designing intelligent systems for programming.
